Privacy Policy for THYS

Effective Date: August 2026

THYS is a mobile application designed to help users take a brief pause and reconnect with the present moment.

Information We Collect

THYS does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

THYS does not require:

User accounts
Registration
Login credentials
Email addresses
Names
Phone numbers
Location data
Device Data

THYS does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, crash reports, or usage statistics.

Reminders and Notifications

If enabled by the user, reminder notifications are generated locally on the device.

Notification settings remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to any server.

Data Storage

Any preferences, settings, or reminder configurations are stored locally on the user's device.

THYS does not operate a backend server and does not transmit user data to external servers.

Third-Party Services

THYS does not use:

Advertising networks
Analytics services
Tracking technologies
Third-party data collection services
Children's Privacy

THYS does not knowingly collect information from children or adults because no personal data is collected by the application.

Data Security

Because THYS does not collect or transmit user data, there is no personal information stored on THYS servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page and reflected by the Effective Date above.
