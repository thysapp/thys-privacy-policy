Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

THYS is a simple reset and mindfulness app. We aim to keep data collection to a minimum and only use information that is necessary to provide and improve the app and its related website.

Information used by the app

THYS does not require you to create a user account. Settings such as reminder preferences and app preferences may be stored locally on your device so that the app can function as expected.

If you enable reminders, THYS uses your device's notification functionality to deliver them. You can change or disable notification permissions at any time in your device settings.

Purchases and Google Play

If THYS offers paid features, subscriptions, or other purchases through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play. THYS does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Google may process purchase and device information according to its own privacy policies.

Website and Meta Pixel

The THYS website may use Meta Pixel to understand visits and actions such as clicking a link to Google Play, and to measure the effectiveness of advertising. Meta may receive information such as browser or device information, page visits, and interactions with the website. Meta processes this information under its own privacy terms and settings.

Third-party services

THYS may rely on platform services provided by Google, including Google Play and Android services. Those services may process technical, device, diagnostic, or purchase-related information independently under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers described above when needed to operate the app, distribute it, process purchases, measure website activity, or support advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Preferences stored locally by THYS can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Data processed independently by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion practices of those providers.

Children

THYS is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system. If the app's intended audience or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when THYS features, services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions concerning THYS, please use the developer contact information provided on the THYS Google Play listing.
